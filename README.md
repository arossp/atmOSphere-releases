# atmOSphere for Mac

**A Mac app for Bluesky and the blogs you follow.**

I built atmOSphere because I wanted a place I could read, pick up where I left off, and get back to things when I was ready. Somewhere I could follow conversations and blogs in the same app, with my keyboard sharing first-class citizenship with my mouse—and a little of the feel of the bulletin boards I dialed into in high school.

atmOSphere is a fully native Mac app. The beta is free and available for Apple Silicon Macs.

**[Read more and see screenshots](https://www.aaronrosspowell.com/atmosphere)** · **[Download the latest beta](https://github.com/arossp/atmOSphere-releases/releases/latest/download/atmOSphere.zip)**

## Bluesky at your own pace

- Keep your place in Following as new posts arrive above it.
- Open a conversation beside your feed.
- Compose posts and threads, attach images, reply, quote, like, and repost.
- Use messages and notifications without leaving the app.

## A reader for the blogs you follow

The Publications section brings your Standard.site subscriptions into the app, including publications on Leaflet, pckt, and Offprint. Keep track of what's unread, then press Space to page through an article and move to the next one.

## Make it feel like yours

Choose from the included color themes, or create and share your own with custom colors and typefaces. Keyboard navigation and a command palette help you move through the app without reaching for the mouse.

## In development: Desk

Desk is where you'll keep articles and conversations you want to come back to, save your reading progress, and follow the discussions happening around them. It's being developed separately and isn't part of the public beta yet.

The beta includes automatic updates through Sparkle, so you can get new features and fixes as they're released.
